ABOUT

From Kathmandu energy
to Sydney momentum.

The person behind the pixels — where I come from, and why I see websites differently.

I’m Yurose Nagar — a web designer & developer with a background in IT, customer experience and visual storytelling. Born in Kathmandu, based in Australia, working where design, technology, business and culture meet.

Before I built websites, I spent years on the other side of them. Customer-facing work taught me what most developers learn late: how real people actually experience technology — where they get confused, what makes them trust a product, and what makes them come back. Every site I build starts from that understanding, not from a template.

I build websites end-to-end in HTML, CSS and JavaScript and ship them with GitHub and Vercel — and I’m deepening that foundation with a Masters in Software Development. Small enough to care about every detail, technical enough to build it properly.

Away from the screen, Formula One keeps me honest. It’s a constant reminder that talent sets the grid, but consistency wins the championship.

And how I use AI: as one part of my workflow, not a replacement for my knowledge. It helps me explore structures, prototype faster and debug — I review, modify and test everything myself. The full story is on the About this website page.
